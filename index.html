Telephone sounds

North American call-progress tones. Tap a preset to play; tap Stop to end.

Presets stop automatically after about 30 seconds.

Standard DTMF row frequencies: 697, 770, 852, 941 Hz. Column frequencies: 1209, 1336, 1477, 1633 Hz.

Special Information Tones (SIT)

Three ascending tones heard before telephone error announcements. Tap to play once.

North American variants: nominal frequencies and durations from Cisco documentation, table 10. No spoken announcements.
